ANZY'S BIRTHDAY WEBSITE — READY TO PUBLISH

FASTEST WAY (NETLIFY DROP):
1. Open https://app.netlify.com/drop in your browser.
2. Drag the ENTIRE Anzy_Birthday_Live_Website folder onto the page,
   or upload the ZIP called Anzy_Birthday_Live_Website.zip.
3. Netlify will give you a live https://...netlify.app link to share.
4. Claim the deployed site with a free Netlify account if you want to keep it.
5. Open the link on your phone to test the flower game, cake, music,
   cats, and Tiny Comfort Corner.

TO ADD YOUR OWN MUSIC:
Put an MP3 named music.mp3 inside static/ BEFORE you upload.
The sound button will use it. Without an MP3, it plays the built-in melody.

TO MAKE CHANGES:
Edit index.html, static/styles.css, or static/script.js and redeploy.

IMPORTANT:
This folder is static and does NOT require Python or Flask on the hosting
service. The local Flask Python project is in the previous ZIP.
All the cat artwork is included. Make sure you have permission to publish it.

Keep the ZIP contents structured this way:
  index.html
  static/
    styles.css
    script.js
    cats/
    favicon.svg
    music.mp3   (optional)

If you use GitHub Pages, upload index.html and static/ at the repo root,
then choose Settings > Pages > Deploy from branch > main > /(root).
